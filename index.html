What is VibeCoders?

A chill place for coders of any level — from first "Hello, World" to production systems. We believe learning is faster (and way more fun) together.

💬

Real help, fast

Stuck on a bug? Drop it in the help channels or open a support ticket — the community and our bot Pixie have your back.

🚀

Ship projects

Showcase your work, get honest feedback, find collaborators for your next side project or game server.

🌍

Open for everyone

Any language, any stack, any timezone. Russian and English speakers welcome — our translator bot smooths the rest.
